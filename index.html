What is SPRING FEST?

Spring Fest, known as SF (motto: TRUE SPIRIT OF YOUTH), is the annual social and cultural festival of the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur held during January in the spring semester.

What happens in SPRING FEST?

Spring Fest is conceived as a platform for the students of IIT Kharagpur to showcase their extra academic skills.

Tremendous participation and aggressive competition are seen in all the events, especially the flagship events of SF.
